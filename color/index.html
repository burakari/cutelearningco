Frequently asked questions

Is the coloring studio really free?

Yes. It is free, with no ads, no sign-up and nothing to buy.

How do I color a picture?

Pick an animal, choose one of the 16 colors, then tap a shape to fill it. Use Undo if you make a mistake.

Can I save or print my picture?

Yes. Use Save picture to download your coloring as an image, or Print to print it.

What age is it for?

It is designed for children about 3 to 8 years old, with big shapes that are easy to tap.

Does it collect my child's pictures?

No. Everything you color stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Can we color on paper instead?

Yes. The same animals are available as free printable coloring pages in PDF form on the home page.
