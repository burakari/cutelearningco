Questions

Good to know

Is ABC Adventure really free?+

Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

What age is it for?+

It is designed for children getting to know the alphabet, usually around 3 to 7. Younger children enjoy it with a grown-up.

Does my child need an account?+

No. Type a name, pick a buddy and play. Each child can have their own profile on the same device.

Where is progress saved?+

In the browser on your device. Clearing browser data removes it. You can switch or reset players in the grown-ups area.

Does it work offline?+

Yes, after the first visit. On a phone or tablet you can add it to your home screen and open it like an app.

Which languages does it teach?+

Three: English, Spanish and Turkish. Pick one when you start and switch any time from the flag at the top. Each language has its own words, stories, quiz and progress. This website is available in the same three languages.

Who reads the words and stories?+

Your device's built-in voice, so it may sound a little different on each phone, tablet or computer.

What is in the video corner?+

Short videos we made for the game: letters, counting, colors, shapes and Simon Says. They are in English, so the corner shows up when you learn English. A video plays on its own and stops at the end; nothing else starts by itself.

Are the printables free too?+

Yes. Download the PDFs and print as many copies as you like for home or class.
